Chocolate Cake Recipe

All of us like cake! Chocolate cake is no exception; we love it too! Here is a neat recipe to make a delicious chocolate cake!

Ingredients:

  • 1 and 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk or buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling water or hot coffee

Procedure:

  1. Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract. Whisk well until combined.
  4. Pour in the boiling water or hot coffee slowly while stirring. The batter will look very thin, which is normal and keeps the cake moist.
  5. Pour the batter evenly into your prepared pans.
  6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Let cool before serving.
