Cheesy Tomato Pizza

Pizza is a tasty food item appealing to all ages! This is a neat recipe for a cheesy tomato pizza that's mouth watering and tasty!

Ingredients:

  • 1 ball of pizza dough (homemade or store-bought)
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1.5 to 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tablespoons grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (for the crust)

Procedure:

  1. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If you have a baking stone or heavy metal tray, place it inside while the oven heats.
  2. Shape the dough on a lightly floured surface or cornmeal-dusted tray by gently pressing and stretching it into a 12-inch circle. Leave the outer edges slightly thicker for the crust.
  3. Add the sauce by spreading it evenly over the center of the dough, leaving a 1-inch border around the edge.
  4. Top with cheese by sprinkling the shredded mozzarella and parmesan uniformly across the sauce.
  5. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and crisp and the cheese is bubbling and slightly brown
