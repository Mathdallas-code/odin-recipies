Cheesy sandwich with ketchup and mayo

Everyone loves a good cheesy sandwich! This sandwich recipe also contains mayo ( mayonnaise ) and ketchup!

Ingredients:

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 2 slices or ½ cup of cheese (cheddar or mozzarella)
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon tomato ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon butter (for cooking)
  • (Optional): chopped onion, tomato, or capsicum

Procedure:

  1. Mix the Sauce: In a small bowl, mix the mayonnaise and tomato ketchup together. You can add chopped vegetables and a pinch of black pepper here if you like extra crunch.
  2. Assemble: Lay out two bread slices. Spread the mayo-ketchup mixture evenly on both slices. Place your cheese slice or grated cheese right on top of the spread. Cover with the remaining two bread slices to close the sandwiches.
  3. Butter the Bread: Spread a thin layer of butter on the outside of each sandwich.
  4. Toast the Sandwich: Heat a non-stick pan or tawa on medium-low heat. Place the sandwich, butter-side down. Toast for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom turns golden brown, then butter the top side, flip, and cook until the cheese melts completely.
